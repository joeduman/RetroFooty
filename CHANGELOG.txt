TEAMBALL 14.0.1 - QUICK GAMEPLAY / VISUAL HOTFIX
============================================

Patch changes (based on the provided Build 14.0 Competitive AI ZIP):

- Goal netting is no longer solid to players; goalposts remain physical.
- Expand arena runoff from 64 to 96 world units so a whole player can pass
  behind the back net within the visible green arena, and return to play.
  The BALL still collides with physical net walls and posts.
- The ball continues moving, spinning, rebounding, and colliding throughout
  the scored-goal celebration. Scoring happens once. The match timer and
  statistics stay paused during the celebration. Park walking does not gain
  a hidden collidable ball.
- 3v3 / 4v4 / 5v5 pitch width and height are each 7% larger (rounded).
  1v1 and 2v2 pitch dimensions are unchanged. AI policy/trainer unchanged.
- Dark outside-pitch canvas band is now field green. The big black rectangle
  behind GOAL, kickoff numbers, practice countdown and FULL TIME is removed.
  Main match HUD and help text backgrounds are also transparent.
- Post-game shot map plots the start and observed path of actual shots,
  highlighting scored shots in gold and stopping their path at the mouth.
  Existing stats, heatmaps, and archived replays are still available.

TEAMBALL - BUILD 05 / 0.5.0

Physics
- Replaced frame-unit movement and ball repositioning with a fixed-step impulse solver.
- Shooting never moves the ball to the far side of a player.
- Mass-weighted contacts, multiple collision passes and short substeps prevent tunneling.
- Physical posts, open goal mouths and full-ball-crossing goal detection.
- Gentle walking touches and stronger running/sprinting contacts.
- Faster sprint and faster stamina drain; exhausted sprint needs a release and recovery.
- Immediate tap shooting, with power from actual approach speed.
- Removed shot charging, lunge/tackle and separate power-shot/pass actions.
- Kept subtle contact spin; rotation does not add speed to the ball.

Bots
- Predictive chasing, dynamic cover, advancing keepers and spread support runs.
- Evaluate passes, goal angles, interceptions and bank passes.
- Escape corners by banking off the wall and stepping back, without moving the ball by magic.
- Seeded choices break mirrored patterns and make simulation tests reproducible.

Game and controls
- Player names, team choice and all match sizes from 1v1 through 5v5.
- Free movement with no forced position selection.
- Mouse and keyboard control, customizable buttons and persistent preferences.
- Separate Controls and Audio & view settings.
- Clean pause/resume and input release when the window loses focus.
- Spectator matches repeat after full time; a visible Build 05 label identifies new code.
- Module-load failures show recovery instructions instead of a green-screen-only match.

Starting and updating
- No npm packages needed to play; Node.js 20+.
- START-TEAMBALL.cmd and STOP-TEAMBALL.cmd, plus saved PowerShell instructions.
- Stop requests are authenticated and target the server from that exact folder.
- Static files are sent without browser caching. Folder structure is preserved in the ZIP.
