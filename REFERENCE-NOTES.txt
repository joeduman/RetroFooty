Retained bot decisions (implemented, not attributed to unseen video frames)
- One primary chaser per team, including the human in that decision.
- Intercept a predicted ball location instead of trailing its current position.
- Approach from the useful contact side of the ball; circle around if needed.
- Compare direct shots, passes to moving teammates and wall-bank routes.
- Keep support players spread and rotate the covering player with play.
- Advance the covering player toward halfway when the ball moves upfield.
- Retreat toward the goal and project incoming shots for interception.
- At a wall, use a physically possible bank angle and back away for the rebound.
- Save stamina for closing distance and impacts; recover instead of sprint-toggling.
- All bots use the same movement, collision and shoot functions as the human.

Current tuning: provisional values chosen from your feedback
In public/js/config.mjs, measured in game-world units and seconds:
Walk 170/s; sprint 330/s (1.94x); stamina drain 42/s (about 2.38s full sprint).
Stamina recovery 25/s after a 0.45s delay. Any positive reserve can be used.
A partially funded last sprint step receives only that fraction of the sprint benefit.
Base shot 480/s plus 0.66 of actual forward player speed; ball cap 760/s.
Ball drag 0.72/s; solid wall restitution 0.75; ball/player restitution 0.55.
Spin decay 1.15/s and curve coefficient 0.095. No automatic control or orbit forces.
Ball radius 8; player radius 18. Acceleration 3800/s^2, braking 4600/s^2.
Direction reversal uses 4700/s^2 while velocity opposes the input.
Held shooting retries on contact after the 0.22s cooldown; there is no charge.
120 Hz fixed match update with collision substeps at no more than 1/240s.
